Reuse Water System – Quick Card

Keep this card next to the washing machine. The Reuse Water System (Spanish: Sistema de Reúso de Agua) reuses rinse water from your last wash to fill the next one.

The screen, the app and the phone alerts use the language chosen in the app (Settings → Language). Spanish is the default. This card gives the English text, with the Spanish screen text beside it.

When you switch it on

A dog chases a butterfly across the screen for about 4 seconds. Then the screen shows REUSE WATER / System ready (Spanish: REUSO DE AGUA / Sistema listo) for 2 seconds, then READY. Pressing START skips the dog.

Every wash

  1. Check the controller’s screen shows READY and Start washer › Press START (Spanish: LISTO, Inicie lavadora › y pulse START).
  2. Start the washing machine program as usual.
  3. Press the button labelled START on the controller and let go (screen: REUSAR Y GUARDAR › Suelte e inicia), or Reuse and save in the app.
    To use tank water but not keep the rinse water: hold START for 2 seconds until the screen shows SOLO REUSAR, then let go; or Reuse only in the app. Step 4 then shows DESAGUE 4 DE 4.
  4. Let it run. The top line shows the step, WASH 1 OF 4 to SAVE 4 OF 4 (Spanish: LAVADO 1 DE 4 to AHORRO 4 DE 4). After the first wash the screen also says about how many minutes are left.
  5. Done when the screen shows DONE (Spanish: TERMINADO) with the litres it reused and saved.

To cancel at any time, press the button labelled STOP, or Stop cycle in the app.

The top line says the step; the message below says what is happening now. A longer message comes in two short parts that change every 3 seconds (shown here with ›).

The app

On a phone connected to the home Wi-Fi, open reusewater.local. (Until firmware 3.1 it was greywater.local: update old bookmarks.)

  • Overview: what is happening now, Start and Stop, the tank, and Wash tank.
  • Water use: how much water you have reused this month.
  • Refresh: use it if the page looks stuck.
  • Settings → Language: Español or English, for the app, the screen and the alerts.
  • View only: if you signed in as viewer, you see only Overview and Water use and cannot change anything. Use the buttons on the controller, or ask the person with the admin sign-in.
  • Settings → Screen font: four letter styles for the controller screen: Hyperlegible (default), Plain sans, Terminal bold, Schoolbook bold. Save, look at the screen, and keep the one you read best.

Phone alerts start with Reuse Water: (Spanish: Reúso de Agua:).

Installer commissioning checklist (firmware 3.9)

Firmware 3.0 has run correctly on the installed system. Firmware 3.1 adds the Spanish texts and the language setting. Firmware 3.2 renames the app to Reuse Water System, adds the start-up animation and a clearer screen font, and moves the optional buzzer from D12 to D1, on the same control logic. Firmware 3.3 makes the screen messages explain themselves in short pages and makes phone alerts more reliable. Complete these checks when installing or replacing a controller.

Hardware

  • Tap flow sensor position: fitted on the machine’s inlet, after the tap and tank lines join. The tank-water fill and the pump cut-off depend on it seeing the pump’s water.
  • Flow sensor wiring: Schottky diode (cathode to the sensor) and 10 kΩ pull-up to 3V3 on D2 and D3; no resistor divider.
  • Noise check: with no water moving, the Service tab’s pulse counts stay still for 2 minutes.
  • Signal level: at D3 while turning the impeller, at least 2.8 V high and at most 0.5 V low.
  • Pump protection: pressure switch or bypass to the tank fitted.
  • Tank-wash valve: on D0, with a 10 kΩ resistor from D0 to GND.
  • Buzzer (optional): active buzzer module, signal on D1 (TX0), VCC on 3V3, GND on GND (it was on D12 until 3.1); switched on in Settings. A short chirp at power-on is normal.

First start

  • Start-up screen: the dog chases the butterfly (about 4 s), then REUSO DE AGUA / Sistema listo for 2 s, then LISTO. START during the dog skips it.
  • Readability: the bold messages can be read from where the household stands.
  • App address: the app opens at reusewater.local; Service shows firmware 3.9.0.

First cycle (watch the Service tab)

  • Screen shows WASH 1 OF 4 with Waiting to fill, then Reusing water or Using tap water, then Washing....
  • The pump turns on when the machine draws water and off within about 0.3 s after it stops (Recent events: “Pump stopped: machine closed its inlet”).
  • Step 1 ends only when the wash drain starts, not before (Machine filled appears first).
  • Step 4 saves water (Filling tank › Ends in …); the fill valve is on and the drain valve off in Valves and pump. When the pump-out ends, the screen shows Low flow › To the drain and the fill valve turns off within about 3 s.
  • DONE shows the litres reused and saved; Water use shows the wash. With the buzzer on: two short beeps.

Firmware features (3.0 to 3.9)

  • Passwords: the app asked for new passwords on first use, and both were changed.
  • View-only user (if the household wants one): Settings → Security → View-only user on, with its own password. In a private browser window, sign in as viewer: only Overview and Water use, with no Start, Stop or Wash tank.
  • Power cut: switch the controller off during step 1 and on again. Expect the start-up animation, then INTERRUPTED on the screen, an alert, and the notice on Overview.
  • Updates: during a cycle, Wireless updates reads Blocked; when Ready, Allowed now. The IDE lists the port as reusewater.
  • Alerts: a private topic created, the phone subscribed, and the test alert received with the title Reúso de Agua: … (or Reuse Water: …). The topic uses only letters, numbers, - and _; tapping the alert opens the app.
  • Time zone: chosen from the list; the clock on the badge is correct.
  • History backup: Download history file saves a file.
  • Wi-Fi setup key: hold STOP for 5 s at READY. The screen shows ReuseWater-Setup and a key like gw-a1b2c3, unique to this board; it is also printed over USB. Join the network with that key. The old shared password greywater works only if the screen is missing.
  • Screen messages: during a wash the message changes every 3 seconds (for example Draining › soapy water); every part is readable and nothing is cut off. From the second wash a min left part appears.
  • Float switches: with the low float down, hold the high float up for 5 s. Expect the alert check level switches and Check float switches in the app (and three long beeps with the buzzer on).
  • START press and hold: a short press shows REUSAR Y GUARDAR and starts on release; held 2 s the screen changes to SOLO REUSAR. Put the label START: pulse = Reusar y guardar · mantenga 2 s = Solo reusar on the panel.
  • Reuse only: with water in the tank, start a wash with Reuse only in the app (or hold START 2 s). Step 4 shows DESAGUE 4 DE 4 and Sin guardar, and Service shows the fill valve off; TERMINADO shows only the litres reused.
  • Screen font: each of the four styles in Settings → Screen font changes the screen after Save; in each style every message has the same letter size and nothing is cut off; the household’s choice is set before handover (after an upgrade from 3.5 the screen starts in Hyperlegible).
  • Language: Settings → Language switches the app, the screen and the alerts; the household’s language is set before handover.
  • Test alert: the app reports Test alert delivered (or the reason it failed).

Accessibility

  • Use the app with VoiceOver (iPhone) or TalkBack (Android): start a cycle, hear the step changes, stop it.
